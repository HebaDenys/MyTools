# Licensing: free for people, paid for business

MyTools is a public, source-available toolbox owned by **Denys Heba (@HebaDenys)**.
The binding terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE), a custom license prepared for this
project. They have not been reviewed by independent legal counsel.

## The rule

- **Private, personal use: free**, with no account, trial expiry or subscription.
- **Business or professional use: paid license required**, including internal
  tools, employees, consulting, freelance work, sole traders and small companies.
- Other organizational use requires a separate agreement too. There is no
  automatic nonprofit, school or government exemption in the default grant.

An employee using it for a personal letter is different from the same employee
using it for employer logs. Running it locally, modifying the code or not selling
the software does not remove the business licensing requirement.

## Obtain a commercial license

Open a [commercial licensing request](https://github.com/HebaDenys/MyTools/issues/new?template=commercial-license.md)
on this repository. Give only the intended use, tool names, approximate user
count and whether redistribution or SaaS is involved. Issues are public: do not
include secrets, private contact details, client data or confidential contracts.
Denys must agree pricing, scope, duration, payment and any support separately.
**There is no checkout, published price, automatic invoice or automatic contract.**
A license is not purchased merely by opening an issue or downloading a build.

## Boundaries

The restriction applies to original MyTools software, not to independent
third-party code under its own license, or to ownership of user data. Publishing
source code makes inspection possible; it does not make commercial use free.
This is not OSI open source. License enforcement is contractual/legal, not DRM:
there is no activation server, telemetry or remote license check.

Previously granted rights are not retroactively revoked. The separate earlier
TrueScrub 0.1.0 distribution remains subject to the terms it was supplied with.
For legal enforceability and commercial contracts, obtain jurisdiction-specific
legal review rather than treating this repository as legal advice.
