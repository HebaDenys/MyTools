# Contributing

Read AGENTS.md and LICENSE first. Personal use is free; business/professional use
requires a separate paid license. Public source does not mean unrestricted use.

Use a focused branch and PR with a problem statement, solution, tests actually
run, risks and screenshots for interface changes. Use synthetic fixtures only.
Run `npm run validate`. Keep modules isolated and dependencies minimal.

## Explicit inbound licensing grant

You keep ownership of your contribution. To let Denys distribute MyTools under
both its personal-use terms and paid commercial agreements, include the following
statement in every contribution PR, and ensure you have authority to grant it:

> I have the right to submit this contribution. I grant Denys Heba a perpetual,
> worldwide, nonexclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free copyright license to use,
> reproduce, modify, distribute and sublicense my contribution, including under
> paid commercial licenses. I grant patent rights I control necessarily infringed
> by my contribution for those uses. I identify any third-party material and its
> license. I understand this does not transfer ownership of my contribution.

Do not submit employer-owned work without employer authority. Maintainers must
verify this statement and license compatibility before accepting outside code.
A DCO/sign-off alone is not a substitute for this explicit sublicensing grant.
The grant covers only the contribution and rights you control, not unrelated work.
Opening a PR does not buy a commercial license for use of MyTools.
